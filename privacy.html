Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

OmniFlow AI Studio ("OmniFlow", "we", "us") provides AI tools for marketing and e-commerce content at https://omniflow-mlku.vercel.app. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we decide why and how your personal data is used.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, received from Google when you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Usage count: how many AI generations you used each day, so we can apply the daily free limit.
  • What you type: the topic or text you enter in a tool. It is sent to our AI provider to create your result. We do not save your inputs or results on our servers.
  • Analytics (only if you allow it): anonymous visit statistics through Google Analytics. This is optional and off until you tick the box at login.

We do not ask for your phone number, address, payment details or password.

3. Why we use it

  • To log you in and keep your account working.
  • To count and limit daily free credits.
  • To generate the content you ask for.
  • To understand how the site is used and fix problems (only with your analytics consent).

4. Your consent

We ask for your consent before you log in. The Privacy Policy and Terms box is not pre-ticked, and the analytics box is optional. You can withdraw your consent at any time: use "Analytics: On/Off" at the bottom of the site for analytics, or "Delete my account" to remove everything.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your data. We use these services to run OmniFlow, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase - login and our database (name, email, daily usage count).
  • Google - Google sign-in, the Gemini AI service (your typed text), and Google Analytics (if you allow it).
  • Vercel - hosting of the website and server functions.

These providers may store data outside India. We may also share data if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Daily usage counts: automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Your typed text and results: not stored by us.

7. Your rights

  • Know what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or complete your data.
  • Delete your data. Log in and tap Delete my account at the bottom of the site. It removes your account and usage data straight away.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Make a complaint. Write to us first at the email below. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

Connections to the site use HTTPS. Secret keys stay on our server and are never sent to your browser. In our database each user can only see their own record, and only our server can change credit counts. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. If there is a data breach

If personal data is leaked or accessed without permission, we will inform the affected users and report it to the Data Protection Board of India, as required by the DPDP Act and Rules, within the time the law allows.

10. Children

OmniFlow is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete the account.
